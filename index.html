The Hand & The Shadow

I find beauty in the imperfections of a single stroke. My work is an exploration of the human soul through the raw, tactile medium of graphite and charcoal. Each piece is a conversation with the shadows, a slow dance of light emerging from the void.

In this digital space, I invite you to step into my dream. Here, time slows down, and the only thing that matters is the emotion captured on paper.
